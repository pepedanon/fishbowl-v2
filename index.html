Who we are

What is FishBowl?

We’re a team of educators, entrepreneurs, and mentors who believe every child has a unique talent, a voice, and an idea worth sharing with the world.

Traditional education often skips the skills kids need to thrive in the real world — financial literacy, public speaking, creative problem-solving. FishBowl bridges that gap with fun, interactive classes where students explore business concepts, discover their passions, and learn to turn ideas into reality. It’s not just a program — it’s a launchpad for future leaders and change makers.

Why entrepreneurship?

When a kid names their own business, prices their own product, and hears a real customer say I’ll take one — something changes. They stand taller. They speak up. They start to see themselves as capable, creative, and confident.

That confidence is the real product. The bracelets and trading cards are just how we get there.

The curriculum

What students learn

Hands-on, grade-adapted, and always pointed at something real.

  • 01

    Financial literacy

    Budgeting, pricing, counting change, and understanding what it costs to run something you care about.

  • 02

    Branding & marketing

    Naming a business, designing a look, and learning how to tell people about it.

  • 03

    Public speaking

    Pitching ideas out loud, talking to real customers, and building the confidence to be heard.

  • 04

    Product design & sales

    Making something real — then selling it at real community markets and showcases.

  • 05

    Creative problem-solving

    Turning “what if” into “here’s how” — iterating when the first idea doesn’t work.

  • 06

    Leadership & confidence

    Owning a project from idea to execution, and mentoring younger entrepreneurs along the way.

Parent questions

Good to know

What ages and grades can join FishBowl?

FishBowl serves students from transitional kindergarten through 12th grade (TK–12), with curriculum adapted to each developmental stage. Elementary students focus on creativity, making, and first sales; older students go deeper into branding, pricing, and pitching.

What will my child actually do in a class?

Students pick an idea based on what they love, then build it into a real small business: naming and branding it, making a product, setting prices, and practicing their pitch. Many students then sell at real community events like markets and kids’ business fairs. Classes are hands-on and small-group — this is not a lecture program.

My child is shy. Is FishBowl a good fit?

Yes — building confidence is the heart of the program. Mentors work in small groups, students move at their own pace, and no one is forced on stage. Public speaking is practiced gradually, and many of our proudest moments come from quiet kids surprising themselves.

My child has an IEP or learns differently. Can they participate?

Absolutely. FishBowl is designed for diverse learning styles, and our mentors support students with IEPs and learning disabilities. If your child needs specific accommodations, reach out and we’ll plan together.

How much does it cost? Are scholarships available?

Thanks to FishBowl Fund — our registered 501(c)(3) — scholarships and supply grants keep the program open to every family. No student is turned away for financial reasons. Contact us and we’ll find a way.
